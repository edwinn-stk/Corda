Where are my tickets?

Your tickets are under My tickets. Mobile tickets show a barcode that becomes scannable 48 hours before the show.

How do I send a ticket to a friend?

Open My tickets and tap Transfer. Nico, our assistant, will help you start it; then pick the ticket and enter your friend's email. Transfers are free and delivered instantly to the recipient's email; the ticket moves to their account as soon as they accept. Transfers are available until 24 hours before the event. After that, tickets can't be transferred and can only be used by the original buyer.

My friend didn't get the transfer email

Ask them to check spam and promotions. They can also log in to Corda with the same email to see pending transfers.

Can an organizer turn off transfers?

Yes. When an organizer disables transfers for an event, it's shown under Ticket info on the event page and the Transfer button is removed from your tickets.

How does resale work?

Tap Sell / Release for resale and choose how many tickets to list. Listed tickets are removed from your account and published on Corda Resale, where anyone can buy them. You're paid when they sell.

Can I choose who buys my resale ticket?

No. Resale listings are public and the first buyer gets the ticket. Listings can't be held or reserved for a specific person. To give a ticket to someone you know, use Transfer.

The event was cancelled or rescheduled

If an event is cancelled you're refunded automatically to your original payment method. If it's rescheduled, your tickets stay valid for the new date.

How do I update my email or password?

Go to your account settings from the avatar menu.

Can I cancel a resale listing?

Only while it's unsold. Once a ticket sells, it can't be recovered.
